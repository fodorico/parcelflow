# Original brief

Brief received from the client (translated from Spanish). It is the source of the requirements;
the practice scope is defined in `../CLAUDE.local.md` and `decisions.md`.

Build a complete platform to manage shipments — local, national and international — with full
logistics control. The solution must include a fully functional responsive website for
customers, couriers and administrators.

## Key features

- Shipment registration: the customer enters origin and destination, chooses pickup and delivery
  time slots and gets an instant quote.
- Real-time tracking of the courier on a map and status history ("picked up", "in transit",
  "delivered").
- Courier app with new job notifications, route optimization, proof of delivery (signature and
  photo) and status updates.
- Admin panel to assign orders, set rates, view performance metrics and manage users.
- Integration with payment gateways, email / SMS / push alerts, and Google Maps or another
  geolocation provider.
- Reports module and data export.
- Secure, scalable and documented architecture.
